How to use a template

  1. Open the template in Canva, CapCut, Photoshop or any editor.
  2. Put your cover art on a layer under the template so it shows through the square.
  3. Change "Your track title", your DJ name and the store link.
  4. Export and post. Use the same art as your preview Reel so fans recognise the drop.
