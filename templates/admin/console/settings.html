Automatic payouts

When on, a DJ's withdrawal is sent straight to their UPI ID or bank account and marked paid when the bank confirms. PhonePe can't send money out, so this uses a payouts provider funded from your bank. A failed transfer puts the money back in the DJ's balance automatically.

{% csrf_token %}
Setup: keys and webhook addresses

Cashfree Payouts (works on Vercel; no fixed IP needed): in Vercel add CASHFREE_PAYOUT_CLIENT_ID, CASHFREE_PAYOUT_CLIENT_SECRET, CASHFREE_PAYOUT_PUBLIC_KEY (the 2FA public key .pem text) and CASHFREE_PAYOUT_ENV=production. Webhook URL: {{ site_url }}/payouts/webhook/cashfree/ (V2 transfer events).

RazorpayX (needs your server IP allowlisted with Razorpay): add RAZORPAYX_KEY_ID, RAZORPAYX_KEY_SECRET, RAZORPAYX_ACCOUNT_NUMBER, RAZORPAYX_WEBHOOK_SECRET. Webhook URL: {{ site_url }}/payouts/webhook/razorpayx/ (payout.processed, payout.failed, payout.reversed).

Test with sandbox keys first, then switch to production keys. Keep enough balance in the payouts account for a week of withdrawals.

DJs tick “Free promo” when uploading and send a clip in Telegram or on the site.
