DMCA safe harbour

MixedMint respect the intellectual property rights of others. We operate as an automated service provider under the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA).

"MixMint follows the 'Notice and Takedown' procedure for digital assets reported as infringing. We do not proactively monitor content but respond immediately to valid legal notifications."

Reporting infringement

To initialize a takedown request, the notification must include:

  • /01 Physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner.
  • /02 Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
  • /03 Links to the infringing tracks or albums on MixMint.
  • /04 Your contact details: email and postal address.

Send a takedown notice

It reaches our team straight away. We act within 24 hours and email you the outcome.
