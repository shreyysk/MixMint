About us

About MixMint

MixMint is a DJ-first digital music distribution platform operating in India. Our mission: every rupee a fan pays should reach the artist it was meant for — no streaming fractions of a paisa, no black-box payouts.

How it works

  1. DJs upload directly. WAV, MP3, FLAC, AIFF or a ZIP, straight from the browser. No Drive links. Previews play only from the DJ's own YouTube / Instagram embeds.
  2. Fans pay one all-inclusive price. The price you see is the price you pay — fees and taxes included, no surprises at checkout.
  3. Secure vault delivery. Files stream through MixMint's signed, expiring links.
  4. Artists get paid. DJs keep {{ dj_share_percent|default:85 }}% of every sale, plus {{ dj_ad_share_percent|default:15 }}% of the ad income from their pages. MixMint keeps {{ platform_settings.platform_commission_rate|floatformat:"-2"|default:"15" }}% (8% for Pro DJs) to cover operations and keeping the system running: payments, secure storage and delivery, support and upkeep. Payouts go to UPI or bank.

Operations

  • Downloads are token-locked, device-bound and byte-verified.
  • 3 downloads included with every purchase (within 7 days, on your first device); after that, re-downloads cost 50%. Failed downloads retry free, and Download Insurance makes re-downloads free.
  • Refunds and disputes are handled in-app with SLA timers; chargeback-risk earnings can be held transparently.
  • Support: file a ticket or ping us on Telegram — fastest response there.
