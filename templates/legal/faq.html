Frequently asked questions

FAQ

Is the price I see the final price?

Yes. Every price on MixMint is all-inclusive — platform fee and taxes are inside the number. No extra charges appear at checkout.

How do downloads work?

After payment you get a personal, expiring download link served by MixMint itself. Originals are kept in MixMint's private storage (with a backup copy in our private vault), and files always arrive as proper downloads, never in-browser streams.

What if my download fails or expires?

Failed downloads retry free. Used links can be re-issued from your library. After a 3-day lock, re-downloads cost 50% — or 0 with Download Insurance.

How do DJs get paid?

15% platform commission (8% for Pro DJs). Earnings land in the DJ wallet and pay out to UPI or bank account above ₹500 with OTP confirmation.

I'm a DJ — where do my files live?

Upload the file directly from the upload page (MP3, WAV, FLAC, AIFF, or a ZIP for album packs). No Google Drive or other links: files go straight into MixMint's private storage and a backup copy is kept in our private vault.

How do refunds work?

Request from your library with a reason. Eligible cases auto-process; the rest are reviewed with a visible status tracker. See the Refunds page for the full policy.

How do I reach a human?

File a support ticket from the Contact page or message us on Telegram — urgent order/download/payout issues get fastest response there.
