Shipping & delivery

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Digital products only

Everything on MixMint is a digital download (audio files and ZIP packs). Nothing is shipped and there are no shipping charges.

When you get it

Right after your payment is confirmed — usually within seconds. Your purchase appears in My library with a Download button, and we email you a receipt{% if platform_settings.gst_charging_enabled %} and GST invoice{% endif %}. Most files are ready straight away. If one needs a few minutes to get ready, we tell you on screen and send the download link by email (and on Telegram if you connect it), so you can come back to My library when it is ready.

If you don't see it

Payments can take up to 15 minutes to confirm with some banks. If it still isn't there, use Recover your download or tap Help — we reply within a few hours. If a payment went through but we can't deliver, you get a full refund (see the refund policy).

Where we deliver

Anywhere with an internet connection. Prices are in Indian rupees (₹) and include all charges.
