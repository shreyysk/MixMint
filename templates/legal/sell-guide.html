1. Get set up

2. Upload & price

Upload the file directly — MP3, WAV, FLAC, AIFF, or a ZIP for album packs. No Drive links; files go straight into MixMint's private storage. Set your own price; the number buyers see is all-inclusive.

Bundles let you sell several tracks together at a better price — great for album packs and genre kits.

Upload a track Create a bundle

3. Build your store

Your store profile is your home on MixMint — banner, bio, links and genre. A finished profile with a cover converts far better than a blank one.

Collaborations let you credit and split a release with another DJ — add collaborators from a track's page so everyone's store gets the exposure and a share.

Edit store profile Download asset pack

4. Grow your audience

Posts announce new drops to your followers. Referrals reward you for bringing other DJs and buyers in. The asset pack gives you ready-made art to post on Instagram and YouTube.

Track what each release and album earns, see your weekly chart and milestones, and withdraw to UPI or bank (above ₹500, with OTP). Need numbers for tax? Export a GST-ready statement any time.
